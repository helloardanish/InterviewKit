## 🚀 Recommended learning path

1. **Start with fundamentals:** Big-O notation, arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, and hash maps.
2. **Understand trees and graphs:** Traversals, binary search trees, heaps, BFS, and DFS.
3. **Learn problem-solving patterns:** Two pointers, sliding window, intervals, backtracking, and binary search.
4. **Practice consistently:** Solve the LeetCode problems and explain the approach and complexity without referring to notes.
5. **Prepare for design rounds:** Study design patterns and understand when each pattern is useful.
